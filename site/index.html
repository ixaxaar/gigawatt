FEATURES Everything is a real engineering constraint

Procure like an operator

A guided planner (platform → how many → who builds it) on top of a full parts bin. Specs, street prices that drift with the AI boom, lead times that stretch when demand spikes.

  • HGX A100 to Vera Rubin NVL144, AMD Instinct, Gaudi, Cerebras, Tenstorrent
  • Vendor frameworks and relationships unlock discounts and allocation
  • Open orders can be cancelled or trimmed, with a restocking fee

Design at the motherboard level

A tray bench with sockets, DIMM banks, GPU baseboards, NICs, NVMe and PSUs, plus a 42U rack elevation. Live validation and a five-year cost advisor.

  • Power, weight and cooling interface are checked before you order
  • Save designs and reuse them across halls

Wire a real supercomputer

Group racks into one machine, auto-design the fabric or hand-tune it, and see what it can train or serve: per-model tokens per second, MFU and time to first token.

  • Fat-tree bisection, scale-up domains, oversubscription
  • MoE and dense models behave differently, as they should

Customers who actually want things

Offers show a live fit verdict and exactly what you still need to buy. Hit Easy Path and the game orders, installs, deploys and assigns for you as parts arrive. Good service earns renewals.

  • Training vs inference, SLAs, ramp windows, three rival operators
  • Sell idle capacity on the spot market, or sign power purchase agreements

Run it, age it, sell it

Staffing scales with racks, liquid cooling and fabric size. Hardware loses value every year and every GPU generation. Know when to replace a rack and when to ride it out.

  • Burn-in and maintenance policy, spares, failures, repairs
  • An investor board reviews your revenue every January; bankruptcy after three months of negative cash; one of six endings and a scored report
  • A chapter card each year and now and then a decision with two honest answers
  • Six bass-heavy chill tracks (lo-fi, dub techno, trip-hop, ambient) that follow the mood of your hall, a hum that grows with it, and a cue for every button and purchase
  • Three save slots, a monthly autosave, and Continue right where you left off
