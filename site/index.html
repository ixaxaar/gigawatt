How it plays

Four moves a month.
A thousand tradeoffs.

01

Design

Pick a platform, or build a tray and rack from CPUs, DIMMs, GPUs, NICs and PSUs. The validator tells you what breaks.

02

Order

Real lead times, allocation caps and vendor relationships. Buy DIY, an OEM rack or a turnkey one.

03

Build

Power, cooling, floor loading, circuits. Air, rear-door, liquid or immersion. Overcommit and racks trip.

04

Lease

Training customers rent a cluster by the GPU-hour, inference pays per million tokens. Miss the SLA and they leave.

02 · Engineering

Design at the motherboard

A tray bench with sockets, DIMM banks, GPU baseboards, NICs, NVMe and PSUs, plus a 42U rack elevation. Power, weight and cooling are checked before you spend a dollar.

  • Live validation
  • 5-year cost advisor
  • Reusable designs

03 · Scale

Wire a supercomputer

Group racks into one machine, design the fabric or hand-tune it, then see what it can train or serve: tokens per second, MFU, time to first token. MoE and dense models behave differently, as they should.

  • Fat-tree
  • Scale-up domains
  • Oversubscription

Hardware loses value every year and every GPU generation. An investor board reviews you each January. Go three months cash-negative and it is over. One of six endings and a scored report.

For the curious: how it is built

Godot 4 front-end

Vulkan, the 3D hall, drawers and controller layer. A Node sidecar speaks a JSON line protocol to the engine (in-process on Android).

Deterministic engine

Every rule lives in TypeScript: power, cooling, fabric, markets. Same seed, same game; golden trajectory tests pin full campaigns bit for bit.

Scripted players

Six personas play full 97-month campaigns and check whole-state invariants every month.

An RL environment

A step/reset interface with a Python wrapper, so agents can learn to run a datacenter.
