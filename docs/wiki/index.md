# GIGAWATT Player Wiki

*A practical guide to building, powering and leasing out an AI datacenter from 2020 to 2028.*

GIGAWATT is a tycoon game about the whole AI hardware stack: you buy real GPUs, NICs and switches from a catalogue of 690+ parts and 100 vendors, design server trays and racks, power and cool a hall, wire a fabric into a supercomputer, and lease capacity to customers who train and serve models. The campaign runs December 2020 to December 2028 and ends with a scored report covering net worth, reliability and scale.

If you are not sure what to do first, read [Getting started](getting-started.md), then follow [Your first hour](first-hour.md) step by step. Every other page digs into one system of the game.

## Where things live in the game

The bottom dock has eight buttons: **Overview** (the 3D hall), **Customers**, **Supply** (Procure and Warehouse), **Facility** (Build and Energy), **Operations**, **Design Lab**, **Supercomputer** and **Codex**. The [Codex](glossary.md) inside the game also has a "Datacenter 101" view that tells you, live, whether you need each concept right now — when this wiki and the Codex disagree, trust the Codex, because it can see your hall.
